About Nova Creator AI

Nova Creator AI is an AI-powered caption generator designed for content creators who produce Instagram reels, cinematic edits and movie based content.

Instead of spending time thinking of captions, creators can simply describe their video and the AI instantly generates multiple engaging captions.

How It Works

  1. User enters a short description of their video
  2. The request is sent to the backend Spring Boot API
  3. The backend connects to Amazon Bedrock
  4. Amazon Nova Lite AI generates captions
  5. Captions are returned and displayed on the website
